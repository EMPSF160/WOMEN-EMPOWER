About AURA for Women

Why we help

Women and girls globally need our support and protection, today more than ever. Violence against women remains a prevalent phenomenon, with new forms of violence, such as gender based violence online, increasingly appearing.

Our history

AURAforWomen started 15 years ago with a women empowerment unit at a community charity for unemployed teenagers. Today AURAforWomen is located across 14 international hubs and conducts regular human rights trainings and life-saving support for women and girls.
